RIFT DEFENSE

CO-OP · DEFEND EACH OTHER ACROSS THE RIFT

THE RIFT

Player 1 builds on the left, Player 2 on the right. The lane on your side carries drones heading for your ally's core, and your turrets only fire on those. If either core falls, you both lose.

TWO DIFFERENT FRONTS

Player 1 guards the long, winding Swarm Lane: lots of fast, fragile drones, ending in a Brood Carrier that bursts into scouts. Player 2 guards the short Heavy Lane: fewer, slower, armored drones, ending in a Juggernaut. Every 4th wave a surge hits one lane (you get a warning a wave ahead). That's when your ally should GIVE you credits.

CONTROLS

Pick a turret in your panel (P1: 123 · P2: 890), then click open ground on your side. Click a turret to upgrade it, right-click to sell. Space pauses, N calls the next wave early, Esc cancels a merge.

XP & MERGING

Turrets earn XP for the damage they deal. A level 3 turret with full XP becomes ELITE (gold ring). Click an elite turret, then click a different-type elite turret of yours nearby to merge them into a hybrid for 200 credits. The second turret's spot is freed up for a new build. Laser + Plasma = Ion Lance (piercing beam), Laser + Cryo = Frost Lance (slows and makes drones brittle), Plasma + Cryo = Nova Mortar (frost bombs that leave freezing fields).
